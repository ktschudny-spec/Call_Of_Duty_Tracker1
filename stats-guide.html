Call of Duty Stats Guide

Kills

A kill is counted when you eliminate an opposing player. Your total kills show how many enemy players you have eliminated during the games represented by your statistics.

The tracker displays a randomly generated demo total.

Deaths

A death is counted when your character is eliminated by an opposing player or another source of damage.

The tracker displays a randomly generated demo total.

Highest Streak

Your highest streak is the largest number of consecutive kills you achieved without dying.

For example, a highest streak of 12 means you eliminated 12 opponents in a row before your streak ended.

How the Demo Works

This website is a demonstration tracker. The statistics are randomly generated and are not connected to an actual Call of Duty account.
