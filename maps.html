Call of Duty Map Guide

This page explains the maps included in the tracker. The images and descriptions are provided to help users identify each map.

Rust

A small industrial map centered around a tall central structure. Its compact layout creates frequent close-range engagements.

Nuketown

A compact suburban map with two houses and a central street. The map is known for fast action and short sightlines.

Shipment

A very small cargo-container map. The limited space creates extremely close engagements and rapid movement.

Terminal

An airport-themed map featuring an indoor terminal area and outdoor sections with different engagement distances.

Raid

A mansion-themed map with indoor rooms, outdoor areas, and multiple routes between major sections.

Highrise

A construction-site rooftop map with open sightlines, indoor offices, and elevated positions.

Firing Range

A military training-range style map with lanes, buildings, cover, and different engagement distances.

Crash

A damaged urban environment built around a crashed helicopter with buildings, streets, and interior areas.

Shoot House

A compact training environment with lanes, obstacles, buildings, and multiple combat angles.

Standoff

A small town-style map with buildings, roads, and central routes supporting several engagement ranges.

Piccadilly

A London-themed map centered around a damaged shopping and transportation area.

Vacant

A compact abandoned-office style map with interconnected indoor rooms, corridors, and a small outdoor area.
